Stick Fiesta – Privacy Policy

Developer: Codex Infinity  ·  Last updated: November 2026

Stick Fiesta ("the game") is a local party game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked hats and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (remove ads, VIP pack, coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity Ads may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On first launch you can choose personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can change this anytime in Settings. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
