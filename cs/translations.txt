##### LANGUAGE: en-GB
App name: Crowd Siege: Army Cannon Run
Short description: Shoot your crowd through ×2 ×3 gates and crush castles! 500 levels, play offline
Full description:
🏰 Fire soldiers from your cannon, multiply them through gates and smash the enemy castle! Crowd Siege is an easy-to-play, hard-to-put-down crowd battle game with hundreds of tiny soldiers on screen.

➕ MULTIPLY YOUR ARMY

🔥 HOT CANNON – A REAL SKILL MECHANIC

👹 GIANT BOSSES

⚔️ CHAMPIONS & CANNONS
Titan, Ninja, Wizard, Dragon and more champions, 11 cannons and 25 army skins. Upgrade fire rate, army size and more.

🎁 SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
Endless Siege mode, Daily Challenge, weekly Star War, season pass road, your own kingdom, chests, lucky wheel, daily rewards, missions and achievements.

✅ Play offline, no internet needed
✅ One-finger controls, short levels
